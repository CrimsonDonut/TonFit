## Short description (max 80 characters)
Log workouts, see your strength gains and keep your streak. Private and offline.

## Full description (max 4000 characters)
TonFit is a simple, private workout log. Pick a program, log your sets, and see right away whether you got stronger than last time.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
- Every exercise shows a percentage change compared with your last session of the same program, and so does the whole session.
- Personal bests and stalled lifts are spotted for you, with short, practical suggestions.
- A yearly heatmap and a weekly streak show how consistent you have been. Set your own weekly goal.

LOG IT FAST
- Start a session, choose a program, tap the exercises you are doing and add sets in a couple of taps. Your last weight and reps are filled in for you.
- Weighted, bodyweight, timed (cardio, planks, wall sits) and sport (basketball and more) exercises.
- Time in and time out are recorded automatically and can be edited.
- Press and hold to reorder exercises. Forgot to log yesterday? Add it from History.

YOUR HISTORY, ORGANISED
- Browse every session by year and month, open any day and edit it.
- Mark rest days without breaking your streak.
- Create your own programs and exercises, or use the starter Push, Pull, Legs, Upper and Full Body.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- Your data stays on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking, and the app does not use the internet.
- Export a full backup or a spreadsheet (CSV) any time, and restore it on a new phone.
- Kilograms or pounds.

TonFit gives general fitness suggestions, not medical advice.
