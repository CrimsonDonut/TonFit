# TonFit Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE]

TonFit ("the app") is a workout log published by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY]. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your workout data stays on your device.

## What the app stores
- The workouts you enter: programs, exercises, sets, weights, repetitions, durations, session times and rest days.
- Your settings: weight unit and weekly goal.

All of this is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage.

## What the app does not do
- It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
- It does not send your data to us or to anyone else. The app does not use the internet.
- It does not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and it contains no third-party services that collect data.
- It does not read your location, contacts, photos or health sensors.

## Backups and exports
The app can create a backup file (JSON) or a spreadsheet file (CSV) of your workouts when you choose Export. The file is created on your device and handed to the Android share sheet. Where it goes after that is up to you, and it is then handled under the policy of the app or service you choose. The app can also restore a backup file you select.

## Fitness information
TonFit gives general fitness suggestions, such as noticing that your progress has stalled. They are not medical advice. Talk to a qualified professional about injuries, health conditions, nutrition or training plans.

## Your control
You can edit or delete any session, exercise or program inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes the data it stored on your device. Because we never receive your data, we cannot access, recover or delete it for you.

## Children
The app is a general fitness log and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
